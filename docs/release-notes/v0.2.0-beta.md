# Yamabiko 0.2.0 beta

Yamabiko (山彦) listens to Japanese audio on your PC and shows subtitles with furigana and an English translation. It runs on your own NVIDIA graphics card, so your audio never leaves your PC.

This beta brings Yamabiko's own speech model, subtitles that show up sooner, and a new look for the subtitles, setup, and Settings. For everything else, see the [changelog](https://github.com/Yamabiko-ASR/yamabiko/blob/v0.2.0-beta/CHANGELOG.md).

## What's new

- **Kodama-1, Yamabiko's own speech model.** It's built on whisper-ja-760M and tuned on Japanese speech. Short replies and interjections come through more often, and it makes up fewer lines when nobody is talking.
- **Faster subtitles.** Each sentence shows as soon as it's settled, instead of waiting for a pause. It's on by default. To turn it off, go to **Settings** > **Timing**.
- **A new look for subtitles.** The Japanese sits on a light strip and the English on a dark bar, next to a banner that shows the genre or series. You can choose **Watch** or **Learn**, left or centered alignment, and which language older lines keep. The browser/OBS page looks the same.
- **Character names that stay the same.** Press Ctrl+Alt+S and find the show you're watching. Its character names keep the same spelling from line to line.
- **A tray flyout.** Click the tray icon to pause, hide the subtitles, change the genre or series, move the subtitles, or open Settings. The icon changes when Yamabiko is paused or something is wrong.
- **Notices when something stops.** If translations stop or your audio device goes away, a notification and a line above the subtitles tell you until the problem is fixed.
- **New setup, Settings, and installer.** You can now change shortcuts in Settings.
- **Support for 8 GB graphics cards.** Setup suggests the **Smaller** tier for them.

Before this release, Kodama-1 was checked against hand-made transcripts of Japanese clips and compared with the model it replaces.

## Update from 0.1.0 beta

Install over your current copy. Your settings stay.

When Yamabiko starts, setup opens again to download the new models: about 6.6 GB, or 5.6 GB on the **Smaller** tier. Its first page shows how much.

The models that 0.1.0 beta downloaded aren't used anymore. They take about 7 GB in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Yamabiko\models`. If setup runs short of space, **Delete old Yamabiko models** removes them. You can also delete that folder yourself.

If you edited `user_overrides.yaml` by hand and a value is now out of range, Yamabiko doesn't start and tells you which setting to fix.

## System requirements

- Windows 11
- An NVIDIA graphics card, RTX 20 series or later, with a driver that supports CUDA 12
- 10 GB of free video memory, or 7 GB on the Smaller tier (8 GB cards, such as the RTX 4060)
- 16 GB of RAM
- About 12 GB of disk space, or 10 GB on the Smaller tier
- An internet connection for the first setup

## Install Yamabiko

Download `yamabiko-0.2.0-beta-setup.exe` from **Assets** below, and run it. This beta isn't signed yet, so Windows might show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, and then click **Run anyway**.

The first time Yamabiko starts, setup helps you pick your audio device and downloads the models.

## Known issues

- Yamabiko understands only Japanese. Audio in other languages comes out garbled.
- Singing is sometimes subtitled as if it were dialogue.
- Very fast or overlapping speech is the hardest case.
- Sometimes another window ends up on top of the subtitles. Restarting Yamabiko brings them back.
- While the browser/OBS page is on, other programs on your PC can connect to it. To turn off the page, set `overlay.browser: false` in `user_overrides.yaml`.

## Privacy

Your audio and subtitles stay on your PC. Yamabiko goes online to download models, to check for updates, and to look up a series on AniList when you pick one.

**Save diagnostics**, in the flyout under **Troubleshooting**, saves your logs and version details to a zip file on your desktop. It doesn't include audio, and it doesn't include subtitle text unless you turned on transcript logging.
