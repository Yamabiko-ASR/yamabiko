efficientsed-fmn10-tf256-kd.onnx is the EfficientSED fmn10+TF:256 advanced-KD
sound event detection checkpoint (fmn10+tf-256_advanced-kd-weak-strong.pt,
release v0.0.1) exported to ONNX with its mel frontend in the graph.
Source: https://github.com/theMoro/EfficientSED at commit
e3449bfd1f4922b81d82410ed842653f462bfca8. The model was trained on AudioSet
(labels CC BY 4.0, Google). Code and weights are under the license below.
